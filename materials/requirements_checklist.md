## Still Needed From the Team

| Requirement Area | What Needs To Be Added |
| --- | --- |
| Customer interviews | real interviews with consent and summarized findings |
| Citations and references | final APA formatting and any additional source support your team wants to use |
| Competitor specifics | named competitors and sourced evidence if you want a more detailed competitor table |
| MVP proof | screenshots, mockups, or prototype evidence from your actual build work |
| Financial assumptions | refined pricing, pilot sizes, and cost assumptions after interviews or technical validation |
| Team passion and selection rationale | your real story of why the team chose this idea over alternatives |

## Fastest Path To A Strong Submission

1. Run 5 to 8 interviews using `customer_research_pack.md`.
2. Replace the placeholder research plan language in `final_report_draft.md` with actual interview findings.
3. Update the assumptions sheet in `financial_model.xlsx` to match your latest pricing and hardware plan.
4. Add any prototype images or architecture visuals to `pitch_deck.pptx`.
5. Tighten citations and formatting into your final report template.
